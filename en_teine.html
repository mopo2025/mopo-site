SAPPORO TEINE · HOKKAIDO

Sapporo Teine

Snowy peaks, just minutes from the city.

Perched on the western edge of Sapporo, Teine combines the convenience of the city with the serenity of nature. Just a short ride from downtown, it’s often called Sapporo’s own “backyard mountain.”

On bluebird days, the upper slopes open to sweeping views of the skyline and Ishikari Bay. The resort’s well-groomed, varied terrain caters to every rider—from absolute beginners and growing intermediates to seasoned skiers chasing steeper lines.

Families will also find a dedicated kids’ zone, making Teine a welcoming spot for everyone—friends, couples, and children alike.

Resort highlights

The resort has 15 trails in two zones. Its official trail mix is 35% beginner, 40% intermediate and 25% advanced, with a longest run of about 6 km. Olympia suits newer skiers and families; Highland offers steeper terrain.

Public transport

Take JR to Teine Station, then board the JR Hokkaido Bus at stop 3 outside the south exit. Last season the trip took about 16 minutes to Olympia, then another 12 minutes to Highland. Check current dates, fares and timetables before traveling.

JR bus and resort access ↗

The reservation-only Big Runs bus connects selected Sapporo hotels with the resort. Last season it offered a round-trip bus and seven-hour lift-pass package, as well as bus-only tickets. Check the new season’s details before booking.

By car

Allow about 40 minutes from central Sapporo or 10 minutes from Teine IC. Parking is free. Winter road conditions affect journey times; choose the Olympia or Highland destination before setting your route.
